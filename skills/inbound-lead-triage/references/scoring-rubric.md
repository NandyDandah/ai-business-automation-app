# Inbound lead triage rubric

Use this rubric only for inbound enquiries supplied by the user. It is a transparent sorting aid, not a calibrated prediction of conversion.

Score each dimension from 0 to its maximum using explicit evidence in the enquiry. Use 0 when the detail is absent; label it unknown rather than treating absence as proof of a poor lead.

| Dimension | Maximum | Evidence to consider |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Need and service fit | 0–30 | The stated problem matches the business's services or offer. |
| Intent and requested next step | 0–25 | The person asks about buying, a quote, a meeting, a demo, or a concrete next step. |
| Timing | 0–15 | A clear and plausible timeframe is stated. |
| Budget readiness | 0–15 | A budget or willingness to discuss price is stated. Do not penalise a missing budget as if it were a refusal. |
| Decision role and completeness | 0–15 | The enquirer explains their role or provides enough context to route the request. |

Add the dimensions for a score from 0 to 100. Do not award points for urgency words alone. Apply these bands as triage labels:

- **70–100 — Respond soon:** strong stated fit and concrete intent; still verify unknowns.
- **40–69 — Clarify fit:** some positive evidence, but one or more decision-critical details are missing.
- **0–39 — Nurture or route:** weak, unclear, or non-sales intent; explain what evidence would change this assessment.

Always show the dimension scores and cite the specific wording or fact that supports non-zero points. If the message is primarily a complaint or support request, classify and route it appropriately rather than treating it as a sales opportunity. A high numeric total must never override a complaint, safety concern, legal issue, or explicit request not to be contacted.
