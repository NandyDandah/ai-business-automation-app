# Business prospect fit rubric

This score measures fit with the user's requested offer and target market, not likelihood to buy. Use only observable facts and cite the source page for each reason.

- 0 to 30: The business matches the requested industry, business type, location, and customer profile.
- 0 to 30: Public evidence shows a concrete reason the user's offer is relevant. For web design, this could be a broken booking path or poor mobile layout; for another offer, use evidence specific to that offer. Do not invent performance results.
- 0 to 20: The business matches additional size or operating criteria the user requested. If ownership, size, or fit cannot be confirmed from a reliable source, mark it unknown and award no points here.
- 0 to 10: An official public business contact channel is listed.
- 0 to 10: Evidence is current and comes from a primary source.

Give one sentence explaining the score and list the source page that supports it. Use “not enough evidence” rather than scoring if the business cannot be inspected. Do not make claims about revenue, customers, ownership, or intent without reliable public evidence.
