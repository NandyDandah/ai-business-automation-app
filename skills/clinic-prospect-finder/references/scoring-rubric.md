# Web-design fit rubric

This score prioritizes research fit, not likelihood to buy. Use only observable facts and cite the source page for each reason.

- 0 to 30: The clinic clearly matches the requested UK location and dental niche.
- 0 to 30: The official website shows a concrete improvement opportunity, such as broken navigation, poor mobile layout, missing clear booking path, or outdated information. Do not invent performance results.
- 0 to 20: The clinic appears to be an independent or locally operated practice based on its own public site. If this cannot be confirmed, mark it unknown and award no points here.
- 0 to 10: A public business contact channel is listed on an official page.
- 0 to 10: The evidence is current and comes from a primary source.

Give one sentence explaining the score and list the page that supports it. Use “not enough evidence” rather than scoring if the official website cannot be inspected. Do not make claims about revenue, patient volume, ownership, or intent without reliable public evidence.
